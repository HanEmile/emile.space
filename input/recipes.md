# Recipes

## Apfelkuchen

[orig src](https://www.zuckerjagdwurst.com/de/rezepte/veganer-versunkener-apfelkuchen)

Scaled, as the orig src uses a 22cm form, I've got a 30cm one

### Take 1

Batter:

* 370 g Vollkornmehl (I used Dinkel 630)
* 280 g Zucker
* 240 g Speisestärke
* 15 g Backpulver (ca. 1 Päckchen)
* 3,5 EL Zimt
* 370 g vegane Butter
* 370 g Apfelmus

40 min at 200º Umluft

Apples:

* 4 Large Pink Lady apples

It does get quite dark up top after half an hour, unsure how to mitigate this

### Take 2

Batter:

* 330 g Vollkornmehl (I used Dinkel 630)
* 280 g Zucker
* 240 g Speisestärke
* 15 g Backpulver (ca. 1 Päckchen)
* 3,5 EL Zimt
* 370 g vegane Butter
* 390 g Apfelmus

Apples:

* 4 Large Pink Lady apples, halved and slightly sliced to produce more surface area

"Glasur"

Oven:

40 min at 180º Ober Unterhitze
