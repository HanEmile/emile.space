# cocktails

## capgemini

- fill glass to about 2/3rds with ice

- 50ml gin / wonderleaf
- 30ml apple juice
- 30ml lemon juice
- 20ml chai

[shake](/cocktails#shaking) in the french shaker and pour out without the ice cubes.

## apple-paloma

- cut the lime at the "aquator"
- press out the lime juice
- take one big glas (320ml)

- 40ml tequilla / apple juice
- 20ml lime juice

- fill with ice (completely)

- 100ml grapefruit juice (fill the rest of the glas)

- add a small slice of the lime
